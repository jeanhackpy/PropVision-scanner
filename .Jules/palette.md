## 2025-03-02 - Native Device Dialers and Accessible Bottom Sheet Interactions
**Learning:** Utilizing a placeholder <button> for phone contact actions limits mobile responsiveness and screen reader clarity. Replacing it with a semantic <a> anchor wrapping a 'tel:' protocol seamlessly integrates native dialer capabilities across devices. Similarly, transforming decorative bottom sheet bars into accessible buttons with focus styles and aria-labels ensures non-visual and keyboard users can easily close contextual overlays.
**Action:** When implementing contact buttons, leverage semantic anchor tags with appropriate 'tel:' protocols. When adding close or toggle controls to bottom sheets or maps, use semantic <button> containers with inset focus rings and descriptive aria-labels.
